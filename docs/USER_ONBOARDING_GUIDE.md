# RNA Lab Navigator - User Onboarding Guide
**For Lab Members - Beta Testing Phase**

## Welcome to RNA Lab Navigator! 🧬

RNA Lab Navigator is your intelligent research assistant designed specifically for our RNA biology lab. It helps you quickly find information from our lab's papers, protocols, and theses with accurate citations.

## Getting Started

### 1. Access the Application
- **URL**: https://rna-lab-navigator-production-ctbr1wtbw.vercel.app
- **Supported Browsers**: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge (latest versions)
- **Device Support**: Desktop, laptop, tablet (mobile optimization coming soon)

### 2. First-Time Login
1. Click on "Login" in the top navigation
2. Use your provided credentials:
   - **Username**: Your lab email (without @domain)
   - **Password**: Provided separately via secure channel
3. On first login, you'll be prompted to:
   - Accept terms of use
   - Change your password (recommended)

### 3. Understanding the Interface

#### Main Components:
- **Search Bar**: Enter your research questions here
- **Chat Interface**: View your conversation history with the AI
- **Document Library**: Browse uploaded papers and protocols
- **Citation Panel**: See sources for each answer

## Core Features

### 1. Asking Questions
The system understands natural language queries. Examples:
- "What is the protocol for RNA extraction from muscle tissue?"
- "What did Rhythm's thesis say about CRISPR efficiency?"
- "Show me papers about non-coding RNA in muscle development"

**Pro Tips:**
- Be specific - mention technique names, gene names, or authors
- Ask follow-up questions to dive deeper
- Request comparisons between different methods

### 2. Understanding Responses
Each response includes:
- **Answer**: Direct response to your question
- **Citations**: [Author, Year] format linking to source documents
- **Confidence Score**: How certain the system is about the answer
- **Related Topics**: Suggestions for further exploration

### 3. Document Types Supported
- **Research Papers** (PDF): Published and preprint papers
- **Protocols** (PDF/DOCX): Lab SOPs and experimental procedures  
- **Theses** (PDF): PhD and Master's dissertations
- **Lab Notes** (coming soon): Shared experimental observations

## Best Practices

### Do's ✅
1. **Start with broad questions** then narrow down
2. **Use scientific terminology** the system understands
3. **Check citations** to verify information
4. **Save useful conversations** for future reference
5. **Report any inaccuracies** using the feedback button

### Don'ts ❌
1. **Don't upload sensitive patient data**
2. **Don't share your login credentials**
3. **Don't expect real-time literature updates** (weekly updates)
4. **Don't use for medical advice**

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues:

**1. "Cannot connect to server"**
- Check your internet connection
- Try refreshing the page (Ctrl/Cmd + R)
- Clear browser cache

**2. "No results found"**
- Try rephrasing your question
- Use more general terms
- Check if the topic is in our database

**3. "Citation not loading"**
- Click the refresh icon next to the citation
- The document might be processing
- Report the issue if it persists

## Data Privacy & Security

### Your Data is Protected:
- **Encrypted Storage**: All documents are encrypted at rest
- **Secure Transmission**: HTTPS for all communications
- **Access Control**: Only authenticated lab members can access
- **No External Sharing**: Your queries stay within our system

### What We Track:
- Usage statistics (anonymous)
- Query performance metrics
- Error logs for improvement
- Feedback submissions

### What We DON'T Do:
- Share data with external parties
- Use queries for training external AI models
- Store personal information beyond login credentials
- Allow public access to lab documents
